Help

Colours on the pans and timeline

Gold is the note to play right now. A soft outline on a pan note means it's coming up next; set it to Off, Subtle or Bold in Setup → Play along. Teal boxes on the timeline are notes you've selected.

Getting your recording in

Go to Setup and tap Choose a recording. It works with MP3, M4A, WAV or a video of the rehearsal. Songs from Apple Music or Spotify can't be chosen because they're locked to those apps; use a recording saved in your Files or Downloads.

Your recording stays on this phone, so it's ready next time you open the app.

For the pan photos, use the full, uncropped pictures so the highlights line up with the real note areas.

Landscape: play first, edit after

Turn your phone sideways and the pans fill the screen.

Play tab: your notes fly in from the right across the top and reach the gold line at the moment you play them; chords arrive stacked.

Write tab: just the pans, a thin progress line and a bubble showing the next note, so you can tap along with nothing in the way. Tap ✎ Edit to slide the full timeline up for fine-tuning, and Done to put it away.

Turn back to portrait any time for the regular layout, including Build a cue.

Practice notes with your stylus

The Notes tab is a page to write on. Pick a colour and write; pressing harder with a stylus makes the line thicker. ⌫ erases whole strokes, ↶ ↷ undo and redo, and ＋ adds a page. ⋯ has Save page as picture, Clear page and Delete page.

As soon as you use a stylus, Stylus only turns on: the pen writes, and your finger or palm scrolls the page instead, so resting your hand won't leave marks. Without a stylus, use ✋ to scroll. Notes save to your account like everything else.

Making a video for the band

Setup → Share → Make a video films the pans lighting up in gold, with your part scrolling across the top and your recording as the sound.

Timeline in the video: Letters shows your part the way you write it (E/C-pan notes orange, B/F-pan notes blue, chords stacked in a dashed box, bar numbers along the top, a wavy line under rolls) sliding past the gold line, and the letter you're on lights up. Boxes shows the note boxes from the Cues timeline instead.

Choose Whole song, your Loop section, or From gold line (wherever the playhead is now), then Start recording. The song plays once at normal speed while the video is made, so a 4-minute song takes 4 minutes. Keep the screen on and Pan Notes open the whole time; if you switch apps, the picture freezes.

Stop and finish ends early and keeps what's been recorded. Then tap Save video. Chrome usually makes an MP4 that plays anywhere; if your phone makes a WebM file instead, it plays on Android and computers, but iPhones may need it sent through WhatsApp or YouTube first.

Panyard: the lanes and your streak

On Play (in portrait above the pans, sideways right between them), your notes fall down two lanes, coral for the E / C pan and aqua for the B / F pan, and land on the glowing line the moment they're played. Chords land across both lanes together.

Play along by tapping the pans while the song plays. Each hit gets Perfect, Great, or Early / Late, and your streak counts up in the corner until you miss one. Your best streak is kept. Jump back in the song and the count starts fresh.

Hearing the pans when you tap

Tap a note on the pans in Play or Cues and you'll hear it, tuned to the double guitar (C♯3 to G♯4). Hold a note to hear a roll, and use two fingers for a chord.

Setup → Play along → Pan sounds: When paused (the usual) plays sounds only while the song is stopped, Always also plays them over the song, and Off keeps it quiet. If you hear nothing, check your phone isn't on silent.

Recording your own pan sounds

Setup → Your pan sounds → Record your pans. Tap a note on the drawing, tap Record, and hit that note once on your real pan. Let it ring; Pan Notes waits for the hit, keeps about 2½ seconds, trims the silence and evens out the volume. It also tells you which note it heard, so you know you hit the right one.

Recorded notes turn green. Next ▶ jumps to the next note you haven't done. Not happy with one? Tap it and Record again, or Delete it. You can also pick a recording you already have with Use a file instead.

Once you've recorded, Sound of the pans switches to My recordings: tapping the pans plays your real notes, holding plays a roll, and any note you haven't recorded uses the built-in tone. Your sounds stay on this phone and go into backup files, so they move with you.

Full screen and keeping the screen on

⛶ Full screen (here and in Setup) hides the browser bars where your phone allows it. Inside the Claude app it may not be allowed; in Chrome it usually works. While a song is playing, Pan Notes also keeps your screen from going to sleep (Setup → Play along).

Tap along and chords

In Cues, choose Tap along, press play, and tap each note on the pans as you hear it. Each tap drops that note on the timeline.

Hold a note to record a roll: it shimmers while you hold, and the roll lasts as long as your finger stays down. Hold two notes for a double roll. A quick tap stays a plain note.

For a chord, tap both notes at the same time with two fingers. Taps within a split second of each other become one chord, and one Undo removes the whole chord.

Slowing the song to 0.75× (the 1× button by the zoom) makes tapping along much easier.

Editing on the timeline

Tap a note to select it. A panel opens with Earlier / Later, Shorter / Longer, Snap, Loop this, Copy, Repeat, Edit and Delete.

Drag a note to move it, or drag its right edge to change how long it lasts. Hold near the edge of the timeline while dragging and it scrolls along.

Pinch with two fingers to zoom, or use − and +. Drag empty space to move through the song; tap empty space to jump there.

Select lets you pick several notes: tap them, or drag across a stretch to grab a whole phrase. Repeat puts a copy a whole number of bars later; Copy then Paste drops it at the gold line.

↶ Undo steps back through any change. Quick taps of Earlier / Later / Shorter / Longer undo together.

Snap and timing

Beat, ½, ¼ snap notes to the beat, half beat or quarter beat. They need the tempo: tap ♩ Timing, play the song, and tap the big button on every beat at least 4 times. Your first tap marks the start of a bar. Use ◀ 10 ms / 10 ms ▶ to line the grid up, and ×2 / ÷2 if the tempo came out double or half.

Hits snaps notes to the drum hits the app finds in your recording (small teal marks on the timeline). It needs no tempo.

With a snap on, Earlier / Later move one snap step (or to the next drum hit), and Snap pulls selected notes into place.

Tap delay fix (in ♩ Timing): if your taps keep landing late, turn it up; if they land early, turn it down.

Building a cue by hand

Choose Build a cue, tap the notes in the order you play them (tap a note again to repeat it), set when it starts and how long it lasts, and save. Use this for exact timings or to add a reminder.

Your part and the abbreviations

"Your part" shows your notes in bars. Letters reads like a written chart: F×4 is four in a row, a dashed box is a chord, · is a short rest, and ×2 after a bar means play it twice. A ↻ line folds up 4 bars you've played before. Tap notes in Letters or Pan tab (a stylus makes this precise) to select them; each tap adds to the selection. A bar appears to mark them r roll, s strum or d drop, add a written note, nudge them earlier or later, or delete them. Rolled notes shimmer on the pans as they play. Pan tab shows one line per pan with notes placed in time. L/R shows where to hit each note. Orange notes are on the E / C pan, blue on the B / F pan. Bars need the tempo (♩ Timing); without it each box is 2 seconds.

out = outer · in = inner · up / low = upper / lower · L / R = left / right side of the pan.

Sharing with your band

Setup → Share. Timeline saves the song in lines with the sound wave, bar lines and your notes at their exact times. Part sheet saves your notes in phrases. Both include a labelled pan diagram. Save as PDF to print, or as a picture for a group chat. Copy as text works anywhere.

A backup file holds all your cues. A bandmate can open it in their own Pan Notes, or you can restore from it. Backups from the old Pan Notes HTML app open too, recording included.

Saving and other devices

Cues, tempo and photos save to your Claude account by themselves and follow you to any device. If a device had different cues, it loads your account's version and keeps its own one Undo away.
