Saved in VR

The band's pans, with your controllers as sticks. Hit harder for a louder note.

In VR: A or X brings the pans in front of you; hold it to hide the studio or bring it back. The studio screen floats above the pans you're at; turn your left palm up for Play, Rec, Learn and Loop on your wrist. Squeeze a grip on the studio screen to nudge it, or with your hand on a pan's rim, to move it. In the pan yard, every pan in your song stands as on a Panorama stage (frontline in front, basses on the wings, drums at the back): push a thumbstick forward, aim the arc and let go to jump to them (or just walk), and push it left or right to turn. To hear a section better, point at it and hold the trigger, then aim where it should go: it slides closer (or further away). Squeeze both grips with your hands on the rims to lift the pans: move, turn and tilt them towards you, so you don't have to look so far down. On the Drum kit, pull a trigger for the kick; the big bass drum stands on your right. Thumbstick up or down: higher or lower. B or Y: the other pans. Press a thumbstick: the menu (pans, look, sound, sticks, the table). On your left, a studio table: tracks, a mixing desk and a track screen. Point a controller at it and pull the trigger.
