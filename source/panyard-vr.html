In VR: A or X brings the pans in front of you; hold it to put the studio table away or bring it back. Squeeze a grip on the studio table to move it. On the Drum kit, pull a trigger for the kick; the big bass drum stands on your right. Thumbstick up or down: higher or lower. B or Y: the other pans. Press a thumbstick: the next look. On your left, a studio table: tracks, a mixing desk and a track screen. Point a controller at it and pull the trigger.
