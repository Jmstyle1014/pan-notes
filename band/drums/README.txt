Panyard's Real kit
Recorded drums and percussion from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) by Versilian Studios,
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). Trimmed, levelled and made
mono at 32 kHz for the app. The kick is made here: a tuned sine body and click over VCSL's concert bass drum.
kit.json lists each sound's note number, its recorded hits and its level.
