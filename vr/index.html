Look

In VR: A or X brings the pans in front of you; hold it to put the studio table away or bring it back. Squeeze a grip on the studio table, or with your hand on a pan's rim, to move it. In the pan yard, every pan in your song stands round a circle: push a thumbstick forward, aim the arc and let go to jump to them (or just walk), and push it left or right to turn. To hear a section better, point at it and hold the trigger, then aim where it should go: it slides closer (or further away). Squeeze both grips with your hands on the rims to lift the pans: move, turn and tilt them towards you, so you don't have to look so far down. On the Drum kit, pull a trigger for the kick; the big bass drum stands on your right. Thumbstick up or down: higher or lower. B or Y: the other pans. Press a thumbstick: the menu (pans, look, sound, sticks, the table). On your left, a studio table: tracks, a mixing desk and a track screen. Point a controller at it and pull the trigger.
